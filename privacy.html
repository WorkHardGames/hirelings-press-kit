Privacy. Last updated 14 October 2026.

Hirelings, by Work Hard Games, sends anonymous gameplay statistics to help us balance the game. It collects no personal data.

What is sent

  • How each battle went: the round and map; your party (characters, classes, levels, stats and abilities), your items and the enemies you faced; what you bought, sold or upgraded in the shop; the result; how many characters survived; how many turns and how much time it took, and how many of them Autoplay played; the damage and healing each character did.
  • How each run ended: the final round, lives spent, gold earned, your final party and items, and your score.
  • Error reports: the error message and where in the game it happened. The game removes your Windows user name, user folder and computer name before sending.
  • The game's version, and whether it is the demo.

Each time the game starts, it makes a new random ID for that session. Nothing is saved on your computer that links one session to the next. All times are in UTC.

What is not sent

Your name, Steam ID, email address, IP address, location, hardware details, or anything outside the game. We cannot tell who you are from these statistics, and we cannot link your sessions together.

Why

Only to improve the game: finding battles that are too easy or too hard, characters and items that are too strong or too weak, and errors to fix. We do not sell or share the statistics, and we do not use them for advertising.

Where and how long

The statistics are stored in a database run by Cloudflare in the European Union. Cloudflare handles the network connection when the game sends data; we do not store IP addresses. We keep the statistics for up to 3 years.

Turning it off

In the game, open Settings ▸ Gameplay and set Anonymous Stats to Disabled. The game then stops sending statistics and deletes any that were waiting to be sent.
